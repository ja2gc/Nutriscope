## Purpose and recording format

Produce a new set of **narrated user manual walkthroughs** using the prepared-form style of the existing demos. Show prefilled examples, briefly explain what staff enter or review, perform the important action and move to its saved result or next step. Keep the existing presentation videos as separate assets.

Keep the written manuals as the complete task references. This file owns the short video sequences, narration cues, handoffs and a fuller scene reference. **The timed cuts below are the editing plan. The 82 numbered scenes later in this file are a coverage/capture bank, not 82 full tutorials to play consecutively.** Combine related shots and explain alternatives over prepared examples.

Start with the role and task. Use populated forms and a few useful section highlights; do not record manually typing every field or repeating the same entry workflow. Capture one relevant edit/save when it explains a feature, and show the actual resulting state. Cut idle loading and repetitive navigation. Keep text readable and narration aligned with the screen. Use chapter markers for the longer RND video.

For each workflow block, name the page to open, explain what staff enter or review in the populated sections, identify the required action, show its saved result and explain the next step. Prefilled values save typing time; they do not replace the explanation of how to do the task.

**Profile and Notifications:** give a short explanation only. Show the Profile sections and describe their purpose; do not demonstrate editing details/photos, changing passwords, verifying recovery email or notification read/dismiss actions. Reports are demonstrated with prepared, genuinely generated outputs and brief source explanations. RND review of a **submitted FSS accomplishment report** is an explicit handoff, including approval and the resulting FSS PDF; unsubmitted drafts are not in that review queue.

**All durations and timecodes are estimates and editing guides, not strict requirements.** Adjust them after narration/rehearsal. Hold longer when a result, graph, report or confirmation needs explanation; shorten repeated actions. Label role/date changes and prepared later states so the edit does not imply that an unsaved action, unfinished purchase or unsubmitted report has completed.

Distinguish these time scales explicitly:

- **Before first use / when references change:** accounts, branding, budgets, Food Library, Inventory, vendors, recipes and templates. Show prepared references and where they are maintained; reuse them in the daily workflow. Explain a creation form without typing every value.
- **Before a service day or purchasing span:** RND's operational menu, shopping list and PO preparation. This can happen before the shift, not necessarily every morning.
- **During the day:** patient visits, FSS menu review, buying evidence, actual served population and daily duty logs.
- **Later visit / end of reporting period:** Monitoring, revised dated plans, accomplishment submission/review and aggregate reports. Show an explicit time transition; do not imply that a follow-up or full reporting period happens within one shift.
