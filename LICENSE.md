## 2. Ownership and institutional use

Upon formal system turnover, ownership of the transferable project rights shall pass to the Partner Institution in accordance with the applicable executed turnover agreements, subject to the retained rights and third-party rights described in these terms.

The Partner Institution may operate, host, copy, back up, maintain, modify, customize, enhance and further develop the system. It may appoint staff or replacement developers to perform those activities on its behalf, subject to applicable confidentiality, privacy and third-party licensing requirements.

The Partner Institution may select its own hosting provider, service plan, domain or subdomain and supporting infrastructure. Changes remain subject to technical compatibility, applicable service terms and protection of institutional and personal data.

The Partner Institution may arrange for designated Provincial Government IT personnel to host and maintain NutriScope for the hospital. Subject to the scope of the rights actually transferred, applicable approvals and third-party terms, the receiving rights holder may further develop and adapt the project for other hospitals. This permission does not represent that the delivered application already supports a shared multi-hospital deployment, nor does it authorize sharing patient records between institutions. Such expansion requires its own technical, access-control, privacy and operational assessment.

## 3. Retained rights

### 3.1. Academic rights

The Development Team and FEU Institute of Technology retain the right to reference, document and present the project for academic and research purposes, including thesis submission, assessment by professors and panelists, university exhibitions, academic presentations and publications. They may retain archival copies of the software and documentation for educational purposes.

These activities shall use fictional or appropriately anonymized demonstration material and shall comply with the confidentiality and privacy requirements in Section 4.

### 3.2. Portfolio and professional attribution rights

Development Team members retain the right to identify NutriScope in their resumes, professional profiles and portfolios; describe their actual roles and contributions; display non-confidential screenshots; and explain non-confidential architecture and implementation decisions during employment interviews or professional presentations.

Public online portfolios may contain those permitted descriptions and screenshots. These rights do not authorize publication of the complete project repository, institutional credentials, confidential technical details or patient records. Descriptions must accurately distinguish the original capstone work from subsequent modifications and must not imply endorsement by the Partner Institution.

### 3.3. Generic skills and reusable code

Development Team members retain the right to use their general programming knowledge, skills, experience, techniques and non-confidential architectural concepts in academic, personal, professional and commercial work.

They also retain ownership of, and the right to reuse, adapt and incorporate into other projects, their own pre-existing, general-purpose code snippets, utilities, templates and boilerplate brought into NutriScope. Examples include reusable authentication scaffolding, API connection helpers, validation utilities and configuration patterns, to the extent the Development Team owns the relevant rights.

This reservation does not extend to hospital-specific workflows, confidential requirements, patient information, institutional branding or the complete NutriScope implementation. Calling a component generic does not override another person's ownership or an applicable third-party license.

For retained reusable components included in the delivered system, the Partner Institution receives a perpetual, nonexclusive, royalty-free license to use, reproduce, maintain, modify and adapt those components as part of NutriScope, including through replacement developers acting on its behalf. Their inclusion shall not prevent the Partner Institution from independently operating and maintaining the delivered system.

Archival software copies shall exclude production secrets and personal data. Demonstrations and screenshots shall use fictional or appropriately anonymized records and omit confidential information.
