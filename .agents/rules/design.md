NutriScope UI Design System

ROLE
Senior UI/UX designer building a modern clinical SaaS platform. Design at a top-tier product level (Linear-quality) with strong focus on usability, hierarchy, and workflow clarity.

DESIGN INTENT
Create a modern healthcare system that is fast, structured, and reliable. It should support complex clinical workflows while remaining easy to navigate and not overwhelming.

VISUAL DIRECTION
Modern SaaS aesthetic with a clinical, data-driven feel. Clean, calm, and professional with a dense but readable layout. Subtle modern styling is acceptable as long as it does not reduce usability or clarity.

UX PRINCIPLES
- Clarity over visual complexity
- Efficient and predictable workflows
- Consistent patterns across all modules
- Minimal friction in repetitive clinical tasks
- Easy comparison of structured data

INTERACTION STYLE
A professional operational tool, not a consumer app. Actions should be direct, purposeful, and consistent across roles and screens.

INFORMATION DESIGN
Prioritize hierarchy and readability in all data-heavy interfaces, especially for clinical and operational data.

ROLE CONSISTENCY
RND, FSS, and Admin interfaces must feel like one unified system while supporting different workflows.

UI CONSTRAINTS
Avoid decorative or marketing-style UI patterns, including unnecessary gradients, glassmorphism, blobs, illustrations, or celebratory effects.

Avoid low-value visual treatments such as oversized decorative icons, pill-style primary actions, or overly stylized stat cards.

Avoid disruptive or noisy UX patterns such as full-page loading screens, unnecessary greeting headers, or optional dark mode toggles.

Avoid implementation inconsistency such as inline styles, arbitrary styling outside system patterns, excessive layering/z-index misuse, or missing tooltips for truncated content.

FINAL STANDARD
The interface must remain structured, clinical, and production-grade with strong hierarchy, consistent patterns, and no visual noise that interferes with usability.
