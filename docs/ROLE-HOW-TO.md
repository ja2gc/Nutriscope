# NutriScope role user manuals

Choose your role below. Each manual contains prerequisites, initial data/setup dependencies, a page directory with navigation labels, numbered procedures, daily and periodic work, and completion checks. These are user instructions for normal operation and staff training.

| Role | Manual | Platform and responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| RND | [RND User Manual](<../Storyboarding/RND User Manual.md>) | Website: patient ADIME/visits, clinical Food Library, operational Inventory/Menu Items, templates, Menu Calendar, procurement, receiving, budget and reports |
| FSS | [FSS User Manual](<../Storyboarding/FSS User Manual.md>) | Android: Home, announcements/SOP, Menu/details, Meal Prep population, purchasing actuals/evidence, daily accomplishments and report submission |
| Admin | [Admin User Manual](<../Storyboarding/Admin User Manual.md>) | Website: accounts, AI usage, announcements/SOP, operational reports, budget, audit, backups/recovery, branding and preferences |

The maintained manuals live in the repository-root **Storyboarding/** directory. This page is their entry point; the full instructions are maintained in the role files rather than repeated here. The [FAQ](FAQ.md) and role-scoped in-app Help answer individual questions. The [installation and turnover manual](operations/README.md) covers technical operators, dependencies, account collection, server commands, production configuration, deployment and recovery.

The RND manual covers the clinical Generate meal plan dialog, Add/Edit carbs across matching recipes, individual slot changes, and saved Show snack slots setting in menus and Oral templates. Menu Calendar and template grids show empty visual positions where a day has fewer items than another day in the same meal row.

## Cross-Role Operational Closeout

1. RND prepares the clinical/operational libraries needed for the task, plans the week and converts reviewed shopping lists into purchase orders.
2. FSS reviews the planned menu, saves purchase actuals and required receipt/proof, records actual served population in Meal Prep and saves daily accomplishments.
3. RND reviews vendor evidence and explicitly marks the vendor received. Uploading evidence alone does not confirm receiving.
4. Eligible purchasing completes only when its actual required work is satisfied. RND/Admin review recorded budget effects and permitted reports; these records are not created by simply viewing a page.
5. FSS submits the accomplishment period once every date has a saved log; RND approves or rejects it with a reason. Admin sees approved reports without the clinical review queue.
6. Admin manages access and oversight. The institution's designated IT/operator manages hosting, credentials, deployment and recovery. The role manual does not grant additional clinical or infrastructure permissions.
