# NCP data meaning

Activity estimates (verified at `e7dea1a9` plus these corrections, 2026-10-30): `Assessment.physical_activity_level` and Monitoring's equivalent retain the five canonical keys. Assessment and Monitoring write them; calculation context, AI evidence, generated summaries and the NCP report consume them. Labels are Sedentary, Lightly active, Moderately active, Very active and Extra active; adult factors remain 1.2, 1.375, 1.55, 1.725 and 1.9. These are local estimates, not measured PAL bands, ICU/stress factors or proof of bed-bound status. Functional Assessment records the latter separately. Legacy bedridden aliases retain their old calculation mapping; report fallback preserves unrecognized recorded text. Existing saved summaries and calculation snapshots are not rewritten. Pediatric category mapping is unchanged; see [calculation basis](../../logic/intervention-goals.md).

Nutrient concerns (verified at `4b2f91de` plus these changes, 2026-10-30): `PesSuggestionService` writes `PesSuggestionState.provider_metadata.concerns` from provider possibilities checked against retrieved cards and cited recorded findings; the diagnosis review API/web consumes `concern_id`, `label`, `missing` and `evidence_used`. These are review suggestions, not diagnoses, measurements or completion evidence; `validated_response` and saved Diagnosis provenance retain their meaning. Assessment fingerprint and library version control reuse; dismissal removes only the selected concern. Reference cards supply no new numerical nutrient standards.

Verified against committed source: `c902f93d6ea742f6303fc62770dfd95683162f14` plus readable evidence presentation in this working tree (2026-10-29).

`PesSuggestionService::response` formats the current, hash-matched cited evidence for both new and cached drafts. Structured nutrients retain their recorded units and zero values; nested recall/lab/context fields use readable labels rather than JSON. Formatting does not rewrite P/E/S, reference hashes, stored Assessment data or provider inputs. See [AI review](ai.md).

`PesSuggestionService` writes `review_contexts.rejected_problems` as failed Problem labels and allowlisted cited field names, never rejected E/S text. The context hash includes those cited fields. A later omission preserves the unresolved Problem; an equivalent valid pending/saved diagnosis resolves it, and relevant evidence changes reopen ordinary review. The service exposes only the boolean `has_retryable_rejections` to the web, which uses the existing Generate action after pending drafts are handled. These are review states, not saved clinical diagnoses. See [AI review](ai.md).

For diagnosis review, `PesEvidenceBuilder` derives weight direction and percentage from Assessment current/usual weight. `PesSuggestionService` can reuse that derived loss when the AI cites both measured weights and their recorded interval; the persisted evidence references retain those original cited fields. A single cited weight does not establish weight change. See [AI validation](ai.md).

A cited adult overweight/obesity classification can reuse its source BMI during eligibility validation. The classifier and existing eligibility checks retain age, edema and maternal-status restrictions; the saved citation remains the computed classification.

The exact derived loss percentage can also be used in signs when both source weights and the interval are cited. Verified E/S wording remains; an unverified paraphrase can use a single concise concerning clause from the cited Assessment instead. Original cited field references are retained. This does not independently verify clinical meaning or causality. See [AI review limits](ai.md).

Purpose: explain important clinical data families and route maintainers to their authoritative writers/consumers. No patient data or independently certified clinical guidance is included.

Automatic screening does not infer unintentional weight loss from a positive recorded percentage. `RiskScoreCalculator::calculate` and the web `deriveRiskScore` leave that two-point factor for the existing RND manual selection. Manual override persistence and the other factor weights/thresholds are unchanged. AssessmentResource exposes recalculated automatic factors; AssessmentController writes the effective score through its existing save path. There is no bulk rewrite of historical records. Verified at `e4b79d5801575723bdaeedd7408728bc28b0322c` plus this screening correction (2026-10-29).

| bmi_classification, bmi_reference_date | Derived read-only API fields, not stored columns. Assessment::classifyBmi uses the cycle date and completed age months: official WHO 2007 sex-specific bands at 61–228 months, Asia-Pacific afterward; month 60 has BMI without classification. WHO cutoffs are strictly below −3/−2 SD for severe thinness/thinness and above +1/+2 SD for overweight/obesity. Offline expanded-table thresholds retain published 0.001 precision in backend/resources/who-bmi-for-age.json and the matching frontend/lib copy; source URLs, retrieval date and workbook hashes are embedded. No interpolation, exact z-score or runtime fetch. Unknown required age/sex, invalid BMI, edema or maternal status withhold class. Ages 0–4 omit BMI in assessment/monitoring writes, API, AI and new reports; older stored values are preserved. Adult IBW/BMR/TEE panel estimates and monitoring IBW targets are withheld through month 228; the prescription engine retains its existing methods. AssessmentResource exposes the reference date and strips neither raw height nor weight; the web strips derived fields from saves. NcpSummaryGenerator prints classification separately from risk; census uses separate pediatric and adult classification buckets. Monitoring AI uses observed_at. PES anthropometric eligibility treats the WHO age range as pediatric, preventing adult BMI/GLIM rules from establishing a pediatric diagnosis |
| Assessment.weight, height, dry_weight_kg, edema_present | Measurement/context inputs; [Assessment](../../../backend/app/Models/Assessment.php), [InterventionCalculationContextService](../../../backend/app/Services/InterventionCalculationContextService.php) maps weightKg/heightCm and chooses the measured/dry weight basis. Trace field_sources/measurement date rather than assuming the assessment is always the newest source |
| muac_mm, waist_cm, hip_cm | Different explicit units in Assessment. Preserve request limits and conversions; do not apply one shared unit by appearance |
| MUAC screening display | [getMuacScreening](../../../frontend/lib/anthropometricSafety.ts) uses known age at the classification reference date and mm: only 6–59 months receive Very low MUAC (<115), Low MUAC (115–<125), or Above screening cutoff (>=125). These are screening observations, not automatic MAM/SAM or adult malnutrition diagnoses. Measurements remain visible for other/unknown ages without a derived label. Above-cutoff values are omitted from the generated RND summary. Pediatric WHO growth z-scores are not automatically calculated |
| food_recall, intake_nutrients, allergies, food_dislikes | Structured clinical inputs in Assessment; [FoodRecallService](../../../backend/app/Services/FoodRecallService.php), [RecommendService](../../../backend/app/Services/RecommendService.php) and [MealPlanService](../../../backend/app/Services/MealPlanService.php) consume relevant food/intake context. Read the selected calculation path before changing the JSON shape |
| Recipe.category | Menu Items use `meal`, `carb`, `snack`, or `tube_feeding`; StoreRecipeRequest validates creation/edits. RecipeController search defaults to Oral categories; explicit `nutrition_support=tube_feeding` selects formulas and `library` permits management of all categories. Oral menu and template writers reject tube categories. Food Calculator uses Food Recall without patient-record persistence |
| NcpRecord.nutrition_support; Intervention.nutrition_support | Single `oral`, `tube_feeding`, or `no_food_menu` choice. AssessmentController writes the cycle choice; missing choices block assessment completion and new intervention saves. A forward data migration fills existing missing choices with Oral. InterventionPlanService captures the cycle choice on creation and protects saved plans from later cycle-mode changes. PesEvidenceBuilder sends the cycle choice as AI context, never independent proof of a problem. PatientMenuPlanGenerator and NcpSummaryGenerator consume saved intervention mode; no-food reports need no weekly menu |
| Intervention.formula_items; MealPlanTemplate.formula_items | Ordered JSON collection, not calendar days or daily intake. FormulaPlanService derives trusted snapshots from existing Recipe calculation, owned source templates/interventions or a validated transient Menu Item. Plan-only creation writes no Recipe/FoodItem. Source UUIDs are checked server-side; snapshots persist independently. Templates can capture a saved intervention; library template profiles only select/remove items |
| Formula portion_multiplier, nutrient_snapshot, original_snapshot | Multiplier applies to the whole recipe; a new library formula starts at 1x. Snapshot nutrients are per serving and ingredient quantities are whole-recipe amounts; displayed totals multiply nutrients by recipe_yield and multiplier. Ingredient edits become the current local baseline, so changing 2x to 3x multiplies displayed current amounts by 1.5. Reset restores original_snapshot at 1x. FormulaCollection and formulaItemsPayload send measured ingredients; FormulaPlanService recomputes nutrients and ignores client totals. Saved displayed_nutrients preserves selected micros even without numeric limits; populated legacy prescription limits also remain visible. Formula selectors show individual macros/micros without summing the collection. Name-only cards reuse MenuEditorSlots; profiles and Tube PDFs show exact planned ingredients. Selected formula totals include fluid via the existing foodFluidBalance estimate from captured water_g; it is not a new daily sum or prescription-scaling target. Oral fluid totals/details share the macro tracker. Multipliers remain editor metadata. PDFs put portion details before macros and every selected micro, marking unavailable data Not recorded rather than zero |
| Oral nutrient_snapshot.recipe_reset_baseline / recipe_reset_quantity | MealPlanItemController captures the original saved recipe and quantity on first edit. MealPlanSection preserves ingredient edits and additions while uniformly changing servings; Reset restores captured amounts. Copying a template/previous menu starts a fresh reset baseline. Food Library and source templates are independent. Missing yields or incompatible measured ingredient units require correction; no invented 1-serving baseline |

[PesEvidenceBuilder](../../../backend/app/Services/Diagnosis/PesEvidenceBuilder.php) includes this narrative as assessment evidence; editing it can reopen relevant diagnosis reviews |

Use [NCP maintenance](README.md) for HTTP owners, tests and domain explanations, and [general data tracing](../platform/data-semantics.md) before editing. Clinical recipes/FoodItem and operational FoodServiceRecipe/FsItem are distinct models; do not merge their units or calculations by field-name similarity.

Before changing clinical data, establish the relevant record shapes, migration history and clinical requirements. This selected map does not enumerate every clinical field; locate additional fields through models/requests and document only the behavior inspected.
