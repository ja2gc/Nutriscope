# Food-service data meaning

Purpose: distinguish operational planning, purchasing, receiving and completed history. It covers both web and FSS-mobile consumers; see [workflow owners](README.md) and [mobile](mobile.md).

## Backend menu fields and snack placement

`MenuItemPicker` offers Food Service recipes and edible catalog items without a meal-slot category restriction. Menu Calendar/templates support ordered multiple lines in any slot; hiding separate AM/PM rows does not prevent placing snacks in Breakfast, Lunch or Dinner. `FoodServiceRecipeController` accepts `meal`, `snack`, `beverage`. Catalog `kind` accepts `ingredient` or `supply`.

`menu_cycle_days.servings_override` is a backend input in the bulk day requests/writer and a costing input in `MenuCycleCostService::aggregate`, ahead of estimated population. Per-slot recipe edits and shopping-list population synchronization clear it; costing simulations also use an in-memory override. The web planner has no independent override control.

`is_event` and `event_allocation` are backend metadata in the bulk day requests/writer, returned by `MenuCycleResource` and captured/displayed in menu audit revisions. The web planner submits false/null and has no event-allocation control. These fields do not drive the costing engine or provide an event-catering UI.

## Seeded food-service references

[food-service.php](../../../backend/database/seeders/data/food-service.php) defines the production purchasing catalog, 50-serving recipes and four September menu-post rotations. `FsCatalogSeeder` creates missing catalog/supplier records; `FoodServiceRecipeSeeder` creates measured ingredient lines and calls `FoodServiceRecipe::recalculateCost`; `FoodServiceMenuTemplateSeeder` creates ordered breakfast/lunch/dinner lines referencing those recipes or edible catalog items. Existing matching food-service records and their edits are preserved. These are reference inputs, not historical service, purchasing, receiving or stock records. Setup and demo commands are maintained in [initial data and seeding](../../operations/initial-data.md).

The Word menu posts control dish identity and meal placement when PPA text differs. Source dates label reusable rotations; weekday assignments are retained, and no September menu cycles or served populations are inserted. Drinks, fruit and desserts remain within the corresponding main meal. Templates contain no AM/PM snack lines. The web's **Show snack slots** checkbox hides or restores those rows and remembers the browser preference without deleting saved lines; clinical snack-generation behavior is separate and unchanged. Menu Calendar PDFs omit empty snack rows.

Catalog quantities/costs use one unit per item: kg for bulk mass, L for liquids, pc for individual products, slice for sliced bread and unit for named supply packs. Recipes use g/mL against compatible kg/L catalog units, or the same count unit. `UnitConverter` handles physical conversions; `units_per_purchase` is not a package-conversion engine. Cans, trays, bags, bottles and bundles with unspecified contents are not treated as measured weights. Their normalized per-unit prices are explicitly estimated, with no asserted package size. Fruit portions are weighed planning estimates; no kg-to-piece conversion is inferred. `Bear Brand Swakto` uses pc, while evaporated milk is a separate recipe ingredient.

All food-service recipe quantities are raw-input planning estimates for 50 servings. The recipe and menu cost services scale them to the target population. Purchase quantities and PPA headcounts inform estimates but do not establish consumed quantities, leftovers or exact dish allocations. Recipe notes identify the estimated baseline. Water/broth preparation amounts may be described in notes without creating purchasing items. Staff review yields, portions, ingredient prices and preparation before operational purchasing.

`Rice`, `Cooking oil`, `Salt` and `Ground black pepper` have `include_in_generated_lists=false`; soy sauce, vinegar and measured sauces remain true. These pantry inputs can remain in recipes but are omitted from generated grocery quantities **and recipe-ingredient costing** under the existing cost service. Calculated recipe costs therefore exclude those pantry purchases; they are not full kitchen expenditure. `Chicken with Lemongrass` retains its dish name and preparation reminder, while lemongrass has no seeded inventory row, measured recipe line, generated grocery quantity or cost. Its absence from purchasing records is not proof of free supply.

The supplier named in the purchasing AIRs is initialized as `Noralyn Macaballug, RPDH-MPC`, without invented contact/address details. Purchased catalog items receive that default supplier even when their normalized unit price is estimated. Supplier evidence and price certainty are separate: additional ingredients without purchase evidence have no default supplier until reviewed. Catalog notes distinguish September reference prices from estimated prices; neither is a claim about the current market. Existing supplier assignments and prices survive reseeding.

## Purchasing and receiving data

Budget mutation ownership is shared by the RND and Admin route groups in [api.php](../../../backend/routes/api.php), both calling [BudgetController](../../../backend/app/Http/Controllers/FSS/BudgetController.php). The two web pages reuse [BudgetPageShell](../../../frontend/components/budget/BudgetPageShell.tsx) with `canMutate=true`. FSS budget routes expose reads only. Fiscal-year setup and manual adjustments change the shared budget/ledger; they are not separate role-specific balances. Existing ledger rows remain immutable. See [Admin operation](../../modules/admin.md#budget-oversight) and [daily role instructions](<../../../Storyboarding/RND User Manual.md#e-budget>).

Shopping-list naming and conversion. `ShoppingList::defaultName` owns fallback names: `Menu Shopping list` with the suggested calendar span, `Shopping list` with the manual procurement date, and `Supplies list` with its procurement date. `ShoppingListController` applies the same fallback for suggested previews, creation, and explicitly blank rename requests; supplied names remain editable until conversion. Resources expose the stored name to web/audit consumers. `PurchaseOrder::displayName` prepends PO, PP, or PPA for order/report listings; the PO number is separate. Existing stored names are not backfilled.

Web creation defaults to `manilaCalendarToday`. Suggested lists require start date, number of days, and positive estimated servings. Missing Menu Calendar plans block both preview and saving, with exact dates shown through the shared save-dialog design. Manual food/supplies require only a procurement date and always display that date, even without `days_span`.

`PurchaseOrderLifecycleService::releaseReadiness` checks suggested estimate/coverage only. Item checkmarks default to included and remain optional exclusions; neither item confirmation, an empty included set, vendor/cost entry, nor budget allocation/balance blocks conversion. `planned_total` sums included rows; `available_budget` remains null for response compatibility, and `fiscal_year` comes from the procurement date. Conversion still copies included rows only, prevents duplicate POs, and freezes the list. Receiving, completion and ledger rules remain owned by their existing services. Regression coverage: `ShoppingListNameTest`, `FoodShoppingListGenerationTest`, `PurchaseOrderReadinessTest`, and web `procurement/page.test.tsx`.

## Purchasing report types and PPA output

Template Edit signatory cards share Name and Position column headings. At narrow widths, the role label spans above the two fields; the fields keep equal, shrinkable columns without truncating role labels. Current values and saved revisions use the same arrangement.

Suggested PPA table rows begin the Menu cell with the calendar day number and align that day's numeric patient count in Output, without dates in the Output column. Total Output is the table footer; Average cost per head a day retains its existing position below the table. Total Cost and Target Date remain shared across the date rows.

`ReportController::instances` validates `procurement_kind` (`all`, `suggested_food`, `manual_food`, `supplies`) for PPA/Procurement Pack feeds before search, covered-month filtering and pagination. All is the initial web selection. A legacy order without a known shopping-list type remains in All rather than being guessed into a food subtype.

PPA supports completed manual food and supplies purchases as well as suggested food. `ProgramProjectActivityGenerator` prints item descriptions, actual quantities/units, one total cost and procurement date for manual/supplies; it omits menu, patient-output and per-head sections. Procurement Pack retains its purchasing forms. Suggested food keeps one aggregate price and prints actual served counts by service date. `PurchaseOrderLifecycleService` captures the date-to-count map in `ProgramProjectActivity.execution_payload.served_populations` when completion freezes output. The generator reads that frozen map first; for older POs it uses the existing locked meal-prep logs only when their sum matches the frozen `actual_output_patients`, otherwise retaining the recorded total without inventing a daily split. The public PO progress shape stays unchanged and is null for manual food/supplies.

The one-time layout-update screen action has been removed. Normal preview never refreshes a final file, and archived or previously archived reports remain filed. For separately authorized maintenance, `PrepareSavedReport::execute(refreshLayout: true)` supports PPA and procurement packs, preserving identity, creation date, parameters and captured branding/signatories through the normal storage path. Appearance versions are `ppa-daily-output-v3-calibri` and `air-reference-v2-calibri`; repeats retain the same file. The HTTP refresh flag remains restricted to PPA and RND/Admin. Maintenance callers must audit the explicit update.

AIR keeps the shared hospital letterhead/logos and existing grid styling, with centered item cells. `partials/acceptance-inspection` renders actual vendor items without padding unused rows. AIR No., PO No., PO Date and Invoice are blank manual fields; the right-hand date retains the recorded purchase date. Date Received/Date Inspected retain the existing purchase-date presentation. Complete/Partial are blank form entries, not new receiving statuses. Signatories are separate unboxed blocks aligned to the content margins. AIR name and title inputs remain editable; visible row labels use the configured position titles, with Conforme retained, rather than Inspected/Verified/Approved-by action labels. Internal role keys and captured historical configurations remain unchanged. The statement positions its Buyer at upper right, Verifier at lower left and Examiner/Approver at lower right, using captured role-keyed names and titles. The uneditable Procurement Pack template card is omitted. Shopping-list resources expose linked PO `display_name`, which the deletion dialog uses instead of an opaque PO number. Naming remains owned by the existing `ShoppingList::defaultName` and `PurchaseOrder::displayName` helpers; conversion locks remain unchanged.

## Vendor receipt total

`PurchaseOrderVendorGroup.receipt_total_override` is nullable money with two decimal places: null means automatic, including existing records; a non-null value means a fixed actual receipt amount, even when it equals the calculated amount or zero. `recalculateTotal()` owns the effective `total_amount`: the override when present, otherwise the existing sum of actual quantity × price (or planned line total when actuals are missing), rounded once to cents. The nullable override is necessary to persist the checkbox mode; it is not another unit price.

The existing vendor-group PATCH accepts `use_calculated_total` and, when false, `total_amount` (non-negative, up to 99999999.99, at most two decimals, matching the existing total column). True clears the override. Omitted mode fields preserve it for older clients. The Resource adds an optional-compatible boolean `use_calculated_total` and retains the existing `total_amount` shape. No unit-price response precision changes.

Web Procurement and mobile `app/(tabs)/procurement.tsx` show **Actual receipt total** with **Use calculated total** checked by default. Editing the receipt amount unchecks it and freezes the amount; checking it resumes live item-total calculation. It never changes item quantities or prices. Per-item **Actual Total** retains quantity derivation. Receipt/OR-only saves omit item values, avoiding resubmission of rounded price displays. Mobile `lib/foodService.ts` does not own procurement requests.

Received groups and completed/archived POs reject receipt-total changes. Existing RND/FSS permissions and evidence rules remain. Whole-group vendor moves carry the effective receipt total into the destination (adding the destination total on merge if either is manual); a partial move preserves the source's fixed amount while rows remain. Completion, per-head calculations and the budget ledger use effective group totals. Procurement packs show a receipt-adjustment row when overridden totals differ from item totals. Stored report bytes and existing records are not backfilled.

Audit revisions capture the mode and effective amount. Older revision groups lacking the mode remain readable and show it as unrecorded. Synthetic regressions live in `PurchaseOrderExecutionLockTest`, web `procurement/page.test.tsx`, and mobile `procurementReceiving.test.cjs`. Establish [test isolation](../platform/testing.md) before execution.

Use [general data tracing](../platform/data-semantics.md) for migrations, Resources, historical snapshots, audit serializers and consumer checks. Inspect applicable writers, installed constraints and balance rules before changing financial data. This selected dictionary is not a claim to cover every operational field.
